The European 24-Hour 1:2:2 Method

Standard 1:1:1 ratios quickly deplete all starches within 10–12 hours. By reducing retained starter to 25g and providing 50g Strong White Flour + 50g water (1:2:2 ratio), there is four times as much food relative to starter volume. In typical European home environments (19°C–21°C), this creates a gentle 24-hour maturation cycle without requiring twice-daily feedings.

🌊 The Float Test

Gently drop a teaspoon of starter into water at 20°C. If it floats buoyantly, the gluten network is trapping carbon dioxide and the starter is at peak baking strength.

🥃 Dark Liquid on Top (Hooch)

A greyish or amber alcoholic liquid on the surface simply indicates your starter has digested all available flour and is hungry. Pour it off or stir it back in, then feed 1:2:2 immediately.

👃 Aroma & Acid Check

Healthy cultures smell pleasant, tangy, and yeasty like fresh apples, sourdough bread, or mild yoghurt. If it smells strongly sharp like nail polish remover (acetone), it is overdue for a feeding.

⚠️ Mold vs. Kahm Yeast

A powdery white, wrinkly film on top is harmless Kahm yeast (skim off and feed). If you see fuzzy pink, orange, green, or black spots, discard immediately and start fresh.

Starter Still Maturing (Day )

Starters require at least 7 days and 7 successful feedings to build the colony density and acidic pH necessary to safely withstand cold dormancy. Refrigerating prematurely may stall young wild yeast development.

Refrigerator Cold Storage (3°C–5°C)

In cold storage, microbial activity slows dramatically. Feed once every 7 days (168 hours).

Steps Before Placing in Refrigerator:

  1. Retain 25g starter + add 50g water (24°C–26°C) + 50g Strong White Flour (1:2:2).
  2. Leave on the counter at room temp (19°C–21°C) for 1 to 2 hours until fermentation initiates and small bubbles emerge.
  3. Cover loosely and place in the main compartment of your fridge (3°C–5°C).
☀️

Reactivate Chilled Culture

Waking up your starter from the fridge for active baking or daily room-temperature maintenance.

Reactivation Instructions:

  1. Take the jar out of the fridge; rest at room temperature (19°C–21°C) for 1 hour to take the chill off.
  2. Retain 25g starter, discard the remainder into your discard jar.
  3. Feed with 50g warm water (26°C) + 50g Strong White Flour (1:2:2).
  4. The app will switch back to the active 24-hour feeding countdown.
